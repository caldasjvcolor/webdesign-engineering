# Local SEO reference

Use for a local service page or location-aware website.

## Entity consistency

Check that the same source-backed identity is used consistently across visible
copy, metadata, navigation, contact paths, and structured data. Keep service
area language precise: an area served is not automatically a street address or
physical storefront.

## Page checks

- one clear primary intent and heading;
- service and audience are explicit;
- location or service area is stated only when supported;
- contact path is visible and usable;
- title, description, canonical, and internal links are unique and coherent;
- business details do not conflict between page, profile, and JSON-LD.

Never manufacture an address, phone number, opening hours, price, review, award,
customer, or service area to complete a local SEO template.
