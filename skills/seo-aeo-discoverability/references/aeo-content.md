# AEO content reference

Use for public content intended to be easy for search engines and answer
systems to understand.

## Content shape

Lead with a specific answer or service description, then add supporting detail,
constraints, process, and a clear contact path. Use descriptive headings,
short factual paragraphs, lists where they clarify, and links to authoritative
sources when relevant.

## Claim discipline

Separate confirmed facts, useful general guidance, and unknowns. Avoid vague
superlatives, fake certainty, keyword repetition, hidden content, and claims
that are not visible or source-backed. An answer system may quote public text,
so clarity and factual accuracy matter more than manipulating phrasing.
