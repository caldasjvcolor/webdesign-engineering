# Technical research

## Purpose

Produce decision-quality knowledge without confusing research with adoption authority.

## When to use

Use for uncertain technical behavior, tool/library/process evaluation or competing approaches.

## When not to use

Do not install, adopt or operationalize a candidate merely because research found it.

## Inputs

Research question, constraints, current system, acceptance measures, source access,
time/cost budget and authority boundaries.

## Process

Prefer primary sources; label each conclusion `SOURCE_FACT`, `INFERENCE`,
`EXPERIMENTAL_RESULT` or `RECOMMENDATION`; record source quality and uncertainty;
for adoption use `CANDIDATE → PILOT → MEASURE → ADOPT / REJECT / DEFER` with an
isolated, reversible pilot when authorized.

## Evidence

Provide sources, dates/versions, experiment method/results, competing evidence,
applicability limits, uncertainty and recommendation rationale.

## Done criteria

The question is answered to the available evidence level and the next decision
or experiment is explicit; no adoption is implied.

## Stop / escalation conditions

Stop when primary evidence is unavailable for a material claim, a pilot needs
new cost/credentials/side effects, or uncertainty remains decision-critical.
